PU (Polyurethane) Injection Services

PU (Polyurethane) injection is an advanced technique for sealing water-bearing cracks and stopping active leakages in concrete structures. PU foam injection is highly effective for emergency waterproofing applications where water is actively flowing through cracks, joints, or voids in concrete.

Service Benefits

Our PU injection services use fast-reacting polyurethane resins that expand on contact with water, completely sealing even active water-bearing cracks. The treatment is suitable for basement walls, retaining walls, tunnels, water tanks, and any concrete structure experiencing active water ingress. Our certified applicators ensure precise injection pressure and material selection.

  • Active Leakage & Crack Sealing
  • Water-bearing Crack Treatment
  • Expansion Joint & Cold Joint Sealing
  • Basement & Underground Structure Waterproofing
  • High-Pressure Hydrophilic PU Foam Injection
