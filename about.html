We are Leaders in Professional Pest Control & Waterproofing Services in India

Established by visionary Alok Gupta, Riyansh Engineering Solution is a trusted provider of integrated Pest Control, Anti-Termite, Waterproofing, and Building Protection Services for residential, commercial, and industrial properties. We specialize in pre-construction and post-construction anti-termite treatments, general pest management, and advanced waterproofing solutions to protect structures from moisture damage, leakage, and pest infestation.

Our solutions are designed to safeguard buildings, infrastructure, offices, homes, and industrial facilities from termites, rodents, cockroaches, ants, mosquitoes, water seepage, and structural deterioration. Using industry-approved chemicals, modern equipment, and eco-safe application methods, we ensure effective, safe, and long-lasting protection.

In addition to pest and termite control, we provide comprehensive waterproofing services for terraces, basements, bathrooms, water tanks, foundations, and other leakage-prone areas. Our moisture-control and structural protection solutions help prevent long-term damage and reduce the risk of termite and pest activity caused by damp conditions.

At Riyansh Engineering Solution, innovation and customer satisfaction are at the core of our operations. We continuously upgrade our treatment techniques, adopt environmentally responsible practices, and customize our services according to site requirements to deliver reliable and sustainable results.

What We Do

We provide integrated pest control, anti-termite, and waterproofing solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial properties.
